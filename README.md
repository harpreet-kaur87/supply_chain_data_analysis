# supply_chain_data_analysis
# Project Title: Supply Chain Data Analysis and Dashboard Development

## Objective:
The objective of this project is to analyze end-to-end supply chain performance across **Sales & Product Performance**, **Manufacturing & Production**, and **Supplier & Logistics** functions using Excel-based data visualization. The project aims to identify trends in product demand, assess supplier efficiency, measure logistics performance, and provide actionable insights for improving cost, time, and quality in the supply chain process.

## Key goals include:

Building a comprehensive Excel dashboard integrating sales, manufacturing, and logistics KPIs.

Analyzing category-wise performance across **Cosmetics, Haircare, and Skincare** products.

Evaluating supplier lead times, manufacturing costs, and logistics efficiency to optimize operations.

Creating a data-driven foundation for improving delivery reliability, cost efficiency, and production planning.


## 📊 Key Findings (Professional Summary for Report or Presentation)

### Product Mix & Revenue Contribution

The dataset covers **3 categories (Cosmetics, Haircare, Skincare) with 100 SKUs** in total.

**Skincare generated the highest revenue of $241,629 (42%)**, followed by Haircare ($174,454, 30%) and Cosmetics ($161,519, 28%).

In terms of sales volume, **Skincare again leads with 20,731 units sold**, reflecting strong market demand.

### Top Performing Products (by SKU)

Cosmetics: SKU38 recorded the highest revenue.

Haircare: SKU51 emerged as the top performer.

Skincare: SKU31 achieved the highest sales contribution.

### Manufacturing Insights

Average manufacturing lead times:

Skincare: 14 days

Haircare: 17 days

Cosmetics: 13 days

Average selling prices: Cosmetics – $57, Haircare – $46, Skincare – $47, indicating premium pricing in the cosmetics segment.

### Supplier & Logistics Analysis

Average shipping times: Skincare – 5 days, Haircare – 6 days, Cosmetics – 7 days, showing faster logistics for skincare.

Average shipping costs: $6 for Cosmetics & Haircare, $5 for Skincare.

Supplier cost analysis:

Skincare: Minimum manufacturing cost – $135 (Supplier 2); Maximum – $701 (Supplier 1)

Haircare: Minimum – $213 (Supplier 1); Maximum – $590 (Supplier 4)

Cosmetics: Minimum – $18 (Supplier 3); Maximum – $311 (Supplier 2)

Indicates potential for supplier cost optimization in higher-cost categories.

### Overall Observations

**Skincare dominates both sales and logistics performance with high revenue share and faster shipping times**.

Haircare and Cosmetics categories show opportunities to reduce production and shipping lead times.

Supplier performance varies significantly by category, suggesting the need for strategic sourcing decisions.
